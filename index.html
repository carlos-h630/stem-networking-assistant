Your 30-Second Opening

15-Minute Coffee Chat Game Plan

A simple game plan to stay confident from hello to goodbye.

Min 0–3: Warm Intro & Agenda Setting
  • Thank them for their time and briefly introduce yourself (school, major/interests).
  • Share why you reached out and what you hope to learn in 15 minutes.
  • Confirm they’re okay with you taking notes for personal use only.
Min 3–12: Asking Core Questions

Pick 3–4 questions from the Search & Connect tab. Prioritize:

  • How they got into the field and what drew them to it
  • What a typical week looks like in their role
  • Skills or experiences that matter most from community college
  • One concrete next step they’d recommend for you
Min 12–15: Wrap Up & Asking “Who else should I speak with?”
  • Recap one insight you’re taking away from the conversation.
  • Ask: “Is there anyone else you think I should speak with to learn more?”
  • Thank them again and mention you’ll send a brief follow-up within 24 hours.
Emergency Lifesavers (if conversation stalls)
  1. “What’s something you wish you had known when you were at my stage?”
  2. “What’s a project or moment in your career that really changed how you think about this work?”
  3. “If you were hiring someone entry-level tomorrow, what would make a candidate stand out?”
